One engine, three views

The result stays
the same.

The recorder, live demo, and Battle Lab share the same simulation. UI themes, overlays, playback speed, and audio change the presentation.

Challenges carry versioned inputs in a link. A worker prepares the replay without blocking the interface. Four-second checkpoints retain the simulation and RNG state, making backward seeks exact.

Built to investigate

A replay is
a test case.

An approach lunge once delayed ability timers. Regression tests now check countdowns and deployments during movement. The same battle can be shared, stepped through, and checked against a fresh run.

Replay fingerprints are diagnostics for this engine and runtime. They are not cryptographic proofs or a guarantee of identical floating-point results across browser engines.
