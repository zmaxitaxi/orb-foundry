# Godzilla Animation Sheet Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: browser game pixel-art sprite sheet for a deterministic 2D canvas game
Primary request: Create an original pixel-art Godzilla-like kaiju animation sprite sheet for Orb Foundry. The creature must be a huge dark charcoal reptilian monster with bright electric-blue dorsal plates, a heavy tail, small glowing amber eye, and a powerful upright silhouette. It must be a new original design, not copied from any film, game, comic, or trademarked character.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background only; no floor, shadow, gradient, texture, border, labels, or text.
Subject: one consistent full-body side-facing kaiju, facing right in every cell, fully visible with generous padding. Keep the same proportions, colors, dorsal plates, head, torso, legs, and tail across every frame.
Style/medium: crisp high-detail pixel art with hard pixel clusters, nearest-neighbor friendly, clean silhouette, transparent-background-ready chroma source.
Composition/framing: exact 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal cells in a single square sheet. One complete centered character per cell; do not let any character cross cell boundaries. Row 1 is four smooth walking frames with alternating planted feet and subtle body bob, tail counter-sway, and no attack. Row 2 is four tail-smack frames progressing from neutral, tail pulled back, tail sweeping forward, to follow-through, with feet planted. Row 3 is four claw-swipe frames progressing from neutral, arms back, one claw extended, to recoil, with feet planted. Row 4 is four atomic-breath frames progressing from neutral, head lifting, mouth glowing cyan, to a short cyan-white beam emitted to the right inside the cell.
Lighting/mood: dramatic blue rim light, restrained glow, readable at small canvas sizes.
Color palette: charcoal scales, steel gray belly, electric cyan-blue plates and effects, amber eye, cream claws, no green anywhere in the subject.
Materials/textures: layered reptile scales and chunky pixel highlights, coherent anatomy, readable feet and tail.
Constraints: exact grid, consistent character scale and facing across all 16 cells, no detached body parts, no extra objects, no floor line, no smoke, no explosions, no text, no watermark.
Avoid: photorealism, 3D render, multiple monsters per cell, cropped feet, cropped tail, inconsistent scale, diagonal top-down view, green subject elements, gradients in the background.
