# Classic weapon generation prompts

## Sawball blade

Create an original pixel-art weapon asset for a browser game: one large, unmistakable circular saw blade, weapon only, centered on a square canvas. Thick crisp black pixel outline, many readable silver metallic teeth, a dark charcoal iron ring, and a brass hub. No orb, no handle, no character, no text, no shadow, and no other objects. Make the blade silhouette large enough to render at roughly 1.6 times the diameter of the plain orb when mounted. Use a perfectly flat vivid chroma green background `#00ff00` for later key removal, with a nearest-neighbor pixel-art look.

Source: `assets/generated/classic-weapons-20260808/source/sawball-blade-chroma.png`

Final: `assets/generated/classic-weapons-20260808/final/sawball-blade.png`

The renderer uses this dedicated asset twice, mirrored by placement and rotated in opposite directions. It does not load the older Orb Race Sawblade asset.
