# Orb Foundry Gameplay Status

## Purpose

This is the handoff document for agents working on deterministic gameplay, physics, abilities, scoring, projectiles, victory, replay state, and settings compatibility.

Do not put canvas-only presentation, DOM layout, or audio playback behavior in gameplay code. Simulation behavior must not depend on the DOM, wall-clock time, frame rate, or `Math.random()`.

## Primary Ownership

- `src/simulation.js`: fixed-step physics, seeded RNG, fighters, abilities, projectiles, collisions, damage, layer progression, events, winner selection, and replay snapshots.
- `src/orb-race.js`: stable Orb Race IDs, racer definitions, categories, team helpers, combo definitions, layer presets, and race geometry constants.
- `src/classic-orbs.js`: the current Classic roster and weapon/combat metadata.
- `src/world-cup.js`: World Cup contenders and mode data.
- `src/king-of-hill.js`: King of the Hill data. Simulation/renderer support exists, but `config.js` does not currently expose it as a selectable/shareable mode.
- `src/config.js`: normalized settings and compact `?cfg=` serialization.

## Determinism Contract

- Simulation advances at `1 / 120` seconds.
- Use the simulation's `SeededRandom` instance for every gameplay roll and randomized spawn/trajectory.
- Cosmetic shake, hit-stop, themes, export FPS, and highlight timing must not affect outcomes.
- Add new mutable gameplay fields to replay snapshots and deterministic comparison helpers.
- Identical settings, seed, and fixed-step sequence must produce identical events and winner.

## Active Modes

### Classic

Classic is permanently 1v1. Its stable IDs are the `CLASSIC_ORB_IDS` export in `src/classic-orbs.js`; the modern path uses fixed mirrored spawns, constant-speed zero-gravity movement, seeded abilities, and the existing winner contract. Orb Race IDs and physics are separate.

The per-orb Classic settings contract is `classicOrbOptions[orbId].orbScale`, with exactly `0.55` (MINI), `0.75` (COMPACT), `1` (REGULAR/default), `1.25` (LARGE), and `1.5` (EXTRA LARGE). `createSimulation` exposes the normalized values as `sim.classicOrbOptions[orbId].orbScale`; there is no Classic `sizeMode` schema. The scale changes fighter radius, mass/collision geometry, copied weapon dimensions, and attached hitbox geometry without changing baseline speed.

The modern Classic baseline is `ORB_BASE_SPEED * 0.75`, exactly 1.5x the previous half-speed implementation. Artist Fairy/Soldier, Bees, and Commander drones self-propel toward the nearest living opponent with normalized velocity; pursuit does not mutate the target. Boxer and Monster are physical, non-chasing orbs with the same own-velocity and directional punch phases. Necromancer soldiers instead patrol on their seeded movement vectors and only aim at the opponent when firing. Target velocity changes are reserved for an explicit impact/knockback or a named ability such as Black Hole.
