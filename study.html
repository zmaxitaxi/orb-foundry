How to read this

The model adds body-contact damage until a layer clears. It assumes equal contact rates and leaves travel time, projectiles, random moves, and live combat out of the calculation. Fuses stay in seconds. Classic fights stay off the bounce chart.

For a selected course, counts use that course’s layer list. The chart continues to show a single layer across health values. Course presets and formulas are David’s November 8 model snapshot, not the current publishing schedule.
